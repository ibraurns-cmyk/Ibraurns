Export Ready

7-Step Export Packaging

Every piece is packed for safe international transit.

1. Cleaning & Inspection — Each piece thoroughly cleaned and inspected.
2. Anti-Tarnish & Moisture Protection — Silica gel packet inside urn and inner box.
3. Protective Polybag — Urn placed inside a protective polybag.
4. Scratch-Proof Wrapping — Soft packing paper to protect the surface.
5. Shock Absorption — Foam or heavy bubble wrap layer.
6. Individual Inner Box — Corrugated inner box, securely taped.
7. Master Carton & Dispatch — Heavy-duty master carton, sealed, labeled and loaded.

Yes. Sample orders of 1–3 pieces are available at production cost + courier.

Can you manufacture from our sample?

Yes. Buyers can provide a physical sample, photograph or design reference. We manufacture to your approved sample or design.

Do you manufacture custom designs?

Yes, subject to technical feasibility. New molds may be required for fully custom shapes.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. International shipping options are available for approved orders via DHL, FedEx or other suitable methods.
